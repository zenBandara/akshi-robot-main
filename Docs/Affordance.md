# Affordance Framework

### A Three-Tiered Design System for Adaptive Interaction

---

## Overview

This framework defines three progressive levels of affordance support — **Low**, **Medium**, and **High** — each building upon the last. Every level is shaped by four core dimensions of affordance:

- 🖐 **Physical** — how the interaction feels and is perceived sensorially
- 🧠 **Cognitive** — how information is structured to reduce mental load
- ❤️ **Affective** — how emotional engagement and motivation are sustained
- 🎛 **Control** — how much agency and flexibility the user is given

---

## Level 01 — Low Affordance _(Default)_

> The baseline. Clean, predictable, and gently engaging. Every subsequent level inherits everything here.

---

### 🖐 Physical

- Sound volume is maintained at a comfortable, appropriate level
- Animation is used purposefully to draw and direct attention
- Backgrounds are kept simple to avoid visual noise

---

### 🧠 Cognitive

- **Four answer options** are presented per question to limit the burden on working memory
- Unnecessary or cluttered graphics are removed from the interface
- The activity structure follows the same flow every time — a predictable routine that reduces cognitive overhead across all questions

---

### ❤️ Affective

- A **gamified reward** is triggered after each correct answer
- The robot displays **animated facial expressions** through its eyes
- A **cheerful voice tone** is used throughout the interaction
- Activity cycles are kept **short** to maintain engagement without overwhelming

---

### 🎛 Control

- The user can **repeat** instructions or **slow them down** at any time
- **Volume adjustment** is available throughout the session

---

---

## Level 02 — Medium Affordance

> Adds sensory amplification, reduced cognitive load, and a richer emotional atmosphere. Everything from Level 01 is retained.

---

### 🖐 Physical

- _(All from Level 01)_
- **Image sizes are increased** for clearer visual perception
- A **soft bell sound** is played as an audio signal just before each instruction or question is delivered — giving the user a moment to prepare

---

### 🧠 Cognitive

- _(All from Level 01)_
- Answer options are reduced from four to **two choices only**, significantly lowering decision complexity
- **Recall after a delay** is encouraged to support memory consolidation

---

### ❤️ Affective

- _(All from Level 01)_
- A full **game-like atmosphere** is applied — including gaming sounds and a gaming-style visual display in the robot's screen and eyes

---

### 🎛 Control

- _(All from Level 01)_
- A **short break option** is made available during the session

---

---

## Level 03 — High Affordance

> Maximum support. Language is simplified, attention is actively directed, and the interaction becomes personal. Everything from Levels 01 and 02 is retained.

---

### 🖐 Physical

- _(All from Levels 01 & 02)_
- **Distinct colors** are applied to differentiate elements clearly
- The **target object is visually highlighted** to guide focus
- Words are **pronounced slowly and deliberately** to aid comprehension
- Only **one focal object** is shown per screen — reducing visual competition entirely

---

### 🧠 Cognitive

- _(All from Levels 01 & 02)_
- All sentences are kept **short and direct**
- Language is **further simplified** to its clearest possible form
- Attention is actively focused using a **pointer or highlight**, chosen based on the individual child's motivation profile

---

### ❤️ Affective

- _(All from Levels 01 & 02)_
- The **child's name** is used during the interaction to create a sense of personal connection and presence

---

### 🎛 Control

- _(All from Levels 01 & 02)_
- The child has the option to **skip a question** entirely, preserving agency and reducing frustration

---

---

## Cumulative Structure at a Glance

Each level is strictly additive — nothing is removed, only layered on top.

```
Level 01 (Low)    →  Foundation: sensory basics, 4 options, gamified rewards, volume/repeat control
Level 02 (Medium) →  Level 01 + bell cue, 2 options only, gaming atmosphere, break option
Level 03 (High)   →  Level 02 + highlights, slow speech, 1 focal object, child's name, skip option
```

---

_Framework designed for adaptive evaluation tasks — particularly in child-robot interaction contexts._
